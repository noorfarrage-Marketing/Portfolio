South Hills, New Sohag

Residential project by TMD (Tatweer Misr Development) · 15-day sales campaign

Sales strategyOffer positioningMeta lead adsOutdoor design
288Kviews on the ad content, August 2026
162messages from the ads

South Hills had a strong offer, but the developer was a new name in the Sohag market. A standard lifestyle campaign of renderings and "luxury living" would have used most of the 15 days building awareness. The brief was to get qualified leads, book site visits and close sales, so I built the proposal around one equation: a strong offer, real proof, a clear reason to buy now, and fast follow-up.

The offer: a 300,000 EGP down payment and the first instalment at handover, with 63, 80 and 114 m² units and instalments from 20,000 EGP a month over five years. The idea turned the usual pitch around: "Take the key first, pay after."

Instead of "apartments for sale", the question to the buyer became: why pay instalments on a home you have not received?

Strategy

The proposal set the funnel from ad to booking (ad, WhatsApp lead, qualification, sales call, site visit, negotiation, booking) and six creative concepts: "The key first", a "before you buy" carousel, a location story with maps and drone footage, a site-staff campaign, and client-review reels. Media budget was split 50% lead generation, 20% video and awareness, 15% retargeting and 15% WhatsApp conversion, and moved weekly on lead quality, cost per lead, appointment rate, visits and bookings.

The clinic needed a page that sells without losing its medical credibility. The plan mixed educational video, before and after results, a map of cases across Upper Egypt and offer posts, with ads built to start a conversation instead of chasing likes.

Across the period the ads brought 2,600 conversations at an average of EGP 6.40 each on EGP 16,400 of spend. The best conversation campaign reached 225 conversations at EGP 6.64 each, and the best awareness ad passed 800K views. About 90% of watch time came from people who did not follow the page, which showed the content was reaching a cold audience.

Reels led engagement with 2.85K interactions against 2.31K for images, and real before and after cases and the regional map performed best. The audience was 55% female and 45% male, mainly 25 to 44, in Sohag, Cairo, Qena and Asyut.

Conversations at under EGP 7 each, from content that reaches people who do not follow the page yet.

What the reports recommended next

Faster replies through automated answers (average reply time was 7 hours, with a 77% response rate), more video campaigns, retargeting for people who showed interest and did not book, and keeping cost per conversation under EGP 7. Targets for the next period: followers up 20%, engagement up 15% and sales up 30%.
